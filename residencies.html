FROM APPLICATION TO ARRIVAL

Six steps, the same for both sides.

01

Apply or post

Pilgrims share their skills. Hubs post their challenges.

02

Get matched

The network connects the right expertise with the right space.

03

The handshake

Scope, dates, and goals are defined by the pilgrim, the hub, and the patron before tickets are booked.

04

Arrive and integrate

Move into the hub, meet the stewards, and learn the local context.

05

Build

Execute the project alongside the hub. You share your methods. The local community shares their reality.

06

Leave it behind

Hand over what you built so the hub can actually use it. You take the experience to your next destination.

The basics

What a residency actually looks like.

Length

Two weeks to three months, set per residency.

Costs

Varies by hub: a room, a stipend, or travel. Agreed up front.

Where

Any participating hub in the Pulsin Network, from Vienna to Buenos Aires.

What you bring

A specific skill the hub needs and a project you can finish in the time.
