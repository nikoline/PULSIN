RUNNING A HUB IS HARD

Doing it alone is a trap. Anyone running a physical space knows the exhaustion that eventually leads to burnout.  We practice a different kind of stewardship where we carry the weight of a physical space with others.

INDEPENDENT SPACES CHOOSING TO STAND TOGETHER

No top-down rules, just a shared safety net, mutual support, and a commitment to keeping each other going.

How the network shows up for your hub

01

Peers who get it

Community calls and one-on-one time with stewards dealing with the same leases, grants and long weeks. Bring what is working and what is not.

02

Your story, documented

The Magazine turns what your hub learned into something the next hub can use, and gives you a record of your own progress.

03

Residents with a mission

Pilgrims arrive through the residency program with a defined project, put their skills to work in your space and leave something behind.

04

Events without the admin

For Plural Events the network handles sponsor outreach and communications, so hosting one does not eat your week.

05

A voice in rooms you cannot reach

When you cannot travel to a conference, people you trust carry your work there, accurately and with your consent.
