The hubs

WE'VE GOT YOUR BACK

Every hub here began the same way. Someone opened a door, kept it open, and found out how heavy a physical space gets.

When one hub needs a lawyer, a grant writer, or an extra pair of hands for a month, another answers.

What is a hub

A PHYSICAL SPACE WHERE IDEAS TAKE SHAPE

A warehouse, a plot of land, a house, with people in it. You run it. You keep it standing.

A makerspace bringing dead land back to life. A hacker house writing open code. A co-living house figuring out how to share a life. A lab on the problem everyone else walked away from.

The money goes back to people, the work stays open. The mission comes first, and the space exists to serve it.
